Common Elixirs

Name Effect
Elixer of Striding This turn, your speed increases by 10 feet and difficult terrain doesn't cost you extra movement.
Elixer of Guile This turn, take a cunning action (dash, disengage or hide).
Elixer of Focussing This turn, if you didn't move, advantage on one attack.
Elixer of Channeling This turn, if you didn't move, the next level 1 spell you cast counts as upcasted by 1 more level. Lasts 1 minute or until used.
Elixer of Health Heal equal to the medicine skill of the crafter.
Elixer of Grace For 1 minute, advantage on all athletics and acrobatics rolls.
Elixer of Vitality Gain temporary HP equal to the medicine skill of the crafter.
Elixer of Weaving The next Opportunity Attack against you has disadvantage. Lasts 1 minute or until used.
Elixer of Bracing +1d4 on your next save. Lasts 1 minute or until used.
Elixer of Coating +1d4 acid damage on your next hit with attack or single target spell that deals damage. If proficient with Alchemist Supplies, add proficiency as well. Lasts 1 minute or until used.
Elixer of Aegis The next time you take elemental damage, half that damage. Lasts 1 minute or until used.

Rare Elixirs

Name Effect
Elixer of Great Strides This turn, your speed increases by 30 feet and difficult terrain doesn't cost you extra movement.
Elixer of Cunning Guile This turn, take two cunning actions (dash, disengage or hide).
Elixer of Sharp Focus This turn, if you didn't move, advantage on one attack and you crit on 19–20.
Elixer of Potent Channeling This turn, if you didn't move, your next spell casts as 1 level higher. Lasts 1 minute or until used.
Elixer of Good Health Heal equal to 2d4 + the medicine skill of the crafter.
Elixer of Swift Grace Until rest, advantage on all athletics and acrobatics rolls.
Elixer of Strong Vitals Gain temporary HP equal to 2d4 + the medicine skill of the crafter. Lasts 1 minute.
Elixer of Intricate Weaving The next attack against you has disadvantage. Lasts 1 minute or until used.
Elixer of Sturdy Bracing +2d4 on your next save. Lasts 1 minute or until used.
Elixer of Deadly Coating +2d4 acid damage on your next hit with an attack or single target spell that deals damage. If the crafter was proficient with Alchemist Supplies, add proficiency bonus as well. Lasts 1 minute or until used.
Elixer of Superior Aegis For fire, cold, electric, acid and sonic each: the next time you take damage of that type, half that damage. Lasts 1 minute or until used.
