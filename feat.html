Crafting Elixirs

You can use these ingredients to craft elixirs.

  • Combine a vial of water, a fresh-found ingredient, and a catalyst (earlier found ingredient).
  • The catalyst needs to infuse the elixir for 8 hours but won't be used up.
  • After the long rest, you'll know what the combination has yielded as a result, if anything.
  • The fresh ingredients and potions must be used within 24 hours before they lose their potency.

Whenever you find an ingredient you haven't used before, you preserve part of it so that you can forever use it as a catalyst later.
